---
title: "Nicknames"
description: "One name for every room, stored on your machine."
weight: 3
---
Press F2 and type:

```
/nick your-name
```

That is your name in the roster, the slot list, remote cursors and solo credits. It is stored on your machine and reused in every room — it is not a room setting, and changing rooms never changes it.

- Names are cosmetic and need not be unique. Roles and ownership always come from keys, shown separately, so nobody can impersonate an owner by picking their name. When two people share a nick, each row also shows a short key fingerprint.
- Names are sealed end to end: the server never sees them.
- If you never set one, your client generates a pronounceable placeholder like `amber-otter` before the first join and keeps it.
