---
title: "Rules & abuse"
description: "What is not tolerated, and where to report it."
---
The server cannot see inside rooms, so rules are about conduct toward the service and other people, not about what you play.

- Do not flood rooms, hammer room creation, or probe join codes. Rate limits and proof-of-work keep the service usable; deliberate abuse gets IP ranges blocked and rooms deleted.
- Do not use invites to harass people. If someone disrupts your room, kick them (`/collab kick`) — the key rotates at once — and revoke their invite.

On a valid report the operator deletes the room by id and may ban IP ranges or raise creation difficulty. Reports cannot be judged by content, only by conduct, because content is ciphertext.
